# Durga Visarjan Brief: Bhopal Ghats

**Purpose:** Where to focus effort at Bhopal immersion ghats for Durga visarjan, based on MPPCB immersion data.

**1. Bairagarh (Sant Hirdaram) kund (HIGH)**
- Dissolved oxygen was below the bathing limit of 5 mg/l in 11 of 13 readings (2023 p.26, 2023 p.60, 2024 p.25, 2024 p.69, 2025 p.28).
- Not tested in Durga 2025. The report has no table for this site.
- Action: Ask MPPCB to sample before (by 18 Oct), during, and one week after (about 27 Oct). Sample inside the kund while it stands.
- Action: Keep immersion out of the open Upper Lake, which has water-supply intake wells (lake report 2023-24 p.1-4).

**2. Malikhedi kund (HIGH)**
- Near-zero oxygen (below 2 mg/l) was recorded in 1 reading, lowest 0.6 mg/l (2025 p.29).
- Not tested in Durga 2025. The report has no table for this site.
- Action: Place aerators in the kund before 20 Oct. Run them for as long as it stands.
- Action: Ask MPPCB to sample before (by 18 Oct), during, and one week after (about 27 Oct).

**3. Rani Kamlapati ghat / Kamla Park kund (MEDIUM)**
- Dissolved oxygen was below the bathing limit of 5 mg/l in 6 of 18 readings (2024 p.70, 2025 p.64).
- In Durga 2025, oxygen was 2.4 mg/l during immersion. It was already 2.5 mg/l before immersion (2025 p.64).
- Action: Place aerators before 20 Oct. Keep them running for the week after immersion.
- Action: Collect idols, flowers and left-over material within 48 hours (CPCB guideline, 2025 report p.23).

**Kund versus river:** Across Madhya Pradesh, oxygen was below 4 mg/l in 11.5% of kund readings vs 2.7% of river readings.

**How ranked:** Ghats were ranked on dissolved oxygen only. BOD and metals were not scored.

**Data gaps:**
- There is no coliform (bacteria) test at the ghats.
- Units are not printed in the MPPCB immersion reports.
- The data cannot separate immersion from sewage as the cause of low oxygen.

**Visarjan date:** 2026-10-20
